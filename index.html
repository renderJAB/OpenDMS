OpenDMS Multi-Physics Engines Comparison Guide
How each real-time WASM physics solver differs in feel, stability, and biomechanics
🦀 Rapier 3D (Default) FASTEST & LIGHTWEIGHT
Rust-based WASM solver using Projected Gauss-Seidel (PGS) with Featherstone articulated dynamics.
• Best for: Real-time authoring, rapid iterations, interactive stunts, and web performance.
• Key Strengths: Instant initialization, zero setup latency, sharp joint limits, rock-solid active pose tracking.
• Motion Character: Snappy, agile character response, ideal for live scrubbing and prompt stunt tuning.
🔬 MuJoCo WASM BIOMECHANICS RESEARCH
Google DeepMind's robotics & biomechanics engine using continuous-constraint convex optimization.
• Best for: Accurate muscle compliance, joint damping, and natural anatomical weight distribution.
• Key Strengths: True Articulated Body Algorithm (ABA), zero joint drift, realistic contact impulses.
• Motion Character: Heavy, organic biomechanical momentum with smooth recovery from stumbling.
⚡ Jolt Physics AAA GAME ENGINE
Modern multi-threaded C++ physics engine developed by Jorrit Rouwe, used in Horizon Forbidden West.
• Best for: High-speed impacts, heavy ragdoll collisions, multi-actor martial arts, and props destruction.
• Key Strengths: Extreme numerical stability under high forces, GroupFilterTable limb isolation.
• Motion Character: Punchy, weighty ragdoll impacts with clean separation between limbs.
🎬 NVIDIA PhysX 5 CINEMATIC VFX & STUNTS
Industry-standard studio physics engine with Temporal Gauss-Seidel (TGS) solver and PCM contacts.
• Best for: Hollywood stunt physics, full-contact falls, high-friction ground stiction, and tumbling.
• Key Strengths: Continuous Collision Detection (CCD) prevents floor tunneling, D6 joint motor compliance.
• Motion Character: Realistic ground rolling, authentic body stiction on contact, and visceral crash tumbling.
💡 Switch active engine anytime via the header dropdown or test presets with different solvers.
